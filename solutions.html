Founder product

What business owners get

01

Funding match engine

Ranked grants, CDFI microloans, city programs, nonprofit support, and coaching options based on location, revenue, funding goal, amount, owner profile, time in business, and credit readiness.

02

Readiness packet builder

A downloadable checklist and action plan covering common documents, use-of-funds details, cash-flow needs, ownership information, and advisor questions.

03

Eligibility watch

Plain-language cautions when a program may have geography, ownership, revenue, time-in-business, or funding-cap restrictions that need to be verified.

04

Trust-first guidance

Clear reminders that matches are not approvals, paid placement should be disclosed, and lower-cost mission lenders should be compared before high-cost alternatives.

How it works

Built for a busy owner who has ten minutes, not ten tabs.

  1. Tell us the basics.Business stage, ZIP code, revenue, goal, amount, and owner profile.
  2. Review ranked paths.See why each program appears and what might block eligibility.
  3. Build the packet.Download the document list and next steps before talking to a lender or counselor.
  4. Apply with context.Use the packet with a CDFI, city office, chamber, SBDC, or nonprofit advisor.
