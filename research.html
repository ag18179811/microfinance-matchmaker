Market case

The wedge is readiness, not another generic loan marketplace.

Small business funding is fragmented across SBA intermediaries, CDFIs, city programs, chambers, and nonprofits. The opportunity is helping owners become ready enough to use those programs well.

34.8M estimated U.S. small businesses
28.5M nonemployer firms, often too small for bank financing
31% full-funding rate cited for firms under $100k revenue
1,400+ certified CDFIs across the U.S. network

Competitive insight

Matching alone is crowded.

Hello Alice, SBA Lender Match, Nav, Lendio, Fundera, LendingTree, CDFI locators, and direct CDFI lenders all help entrepreneurs find capital in different ways. Microfinance Matchmaker should not compete only on breadth.

The stronger startup position is the readiness layer: document preparation, eligibility cautions, and partner-ready intake that makes public and mission-driven programs easier to use.

Operating principles

What the research changes about the product

Keep trust visible

Borrowers need to know when a result is a fit signal, when a rule is uncertain, and when a program may not be available to them.

Build for rule changes

Eligibility can shift across federal, city, nonprofit, and lender programs. The product must flag uncertainty rather than overpromise.

Sell readiness to institutions

CDFIs, chambers, and city programs benefit when applicants arrive with cleaner documents and clearer intent.
