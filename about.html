Mission

Make small business funding less intimidating and more equitable.

Microfinance Matchmaker exists for entrepreneurs who know they need capital or coaching, but do not have time to decode dozens of local programs, lender pages, and application checklists alone.

Our promise

The platform should feel like a calm first advisor.

We are not a lender, broker, or approval engine. We are a readiness and routing layer that helps owners understand where to start, what to gather, and what to ask before applying.

The long-term vision is a verified program network where city agencies, CDFIs, chambers, and nonprofits maintain accurate program profiles while entrepreneurs keep control of their information.

Trust principles

How this should grow

Disclose incentives

If a partner pays for placement, the platform should label it clearly and still show the best unpaid fit.

Protect applicants

Do not sell sensitive applicant data. Let owners decide when to share a packet with a program or advisor.

Prefer responsible capital

Route toward CDFIs, public programs, and nonprofit support before high-cost or opaque financing.

Keep rules current

Eligibility notes must be maintained and written cautiously so the tool does not send people into the wrong application.

Get involved

Building the first partner network

Founders can use the tool today as a planning guide. Organizations can request a pilot to add real program data, intake fields, and reporting.
